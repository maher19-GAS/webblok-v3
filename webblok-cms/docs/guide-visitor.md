# Public Visitor Guide

This short guide is for **anyone visiting a website built with WebBlok**. You don't need
an account, and there's nothing to install — sites are ordinary, fast web pages. Here's
what to expect.

---

## 1. Just browse — it's a normal website

A WebBlok site is rendered as standard HTML/CSS/JS. You navigate it like any other site:

- Click links and menu items to move between pages.
- Pages have clean URLs (e.g. `/about`, `/services/design`).
- The homepage is served at `/`.

No special browser, plugin, or login is required to read public content.

---

## 2. Reaching the right site

WebBlok hosts many sites. Each one is reached by:

- its **own custom domain** (e.g. `acme.com`), or
- a **subdomain** (e.g. `acme.webblok.app`).

You'll almost always arrive via a link someone shared — just open it.

---

## 3. Choosing your language

If a site is published in more than one language, look for a **language switcher**
(usually in the header or footer). Pick your language and the page content updates.

- For right-to-left languages (e.g. Arabic, Hebrew), the layout automatically flips to
  read right-to-left.
- Your choice typically applies as you move between pages.

---

## 4. Interacting with the page

Depending on what the site owner built, you may see:

- **Buttons & links** — go to other pages or external sites.
- **Image galleries** — click to view photos.
- **Contact forms** — fill in your details and submit to reach the site owner.
- **Embedded content** — stats, feature lists, calls-to-action, and more.

Some content can be **live** (it pulls the latest data when the page loads), so you
always see up-to-date information without refreshing manually.

---

## 5. Using a contact form

If the site has a contact form:

1. Fill in the fields (name, email, message, etc.).
2. Required fields are marked — complete them before submitting.
3. Click **Submit**. You'll see a confirmation when it's sent.

Only enter information you're comfortable sharing with the site owner.

---

## 6. Privacy & safety

- WebBlok sites are built from **sandboxed components** — community-contributed content
  is checked to block scripts and unsafe code before it can ever appear on a page.
- As with any website, only submit personal details through forms you trust, and check
  the site's own privacy notice for how your data is used.

---

## 7. Something not working?

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| **404 / page not found** | The page may have moved or been unpublished — go back to the homepage. |
| **403 / not available** | The site may be temporarily suspended — try again later. |
| **410 / gone** | The site has been removed. |
| A form won't submit | Check required fields; try again, or contact the owner another way. |
| Content looks out of date | Refresh the page to re-pull live content. |

These messages come from the platform, not from you doing anything wrong.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to… | How |
|------------|-----|
| Read the site | Just open the link and browse |
| Switch language | Use the **language switcher** in the header/footer |
| Contact the owner | Fill in and submit the **contact form** |
| Get back to start | Click the logo or go to `/` |
