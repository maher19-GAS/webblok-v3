# Chapter Four — Type 3: The Galactic Civilization — Read Through "Be Wiser" and "Be Kinder"

> "And whoever is given wisdom has certainly been given much good." — Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:269
> "And cooperate in righteousness and piety." — Qur'an, Al-Ma'idah 5:2

— — —

## 1) Type 3, Briefly

A Type 3 civilization harvests the energy of a full galaxy — a hundred billion stars. It moves stars with stellar engines. It extracts energy from black holes through Hawking radiation. It lays galactic highways made of stable wormholes. It may meet other civilizations. It may form what the imagination calls a "galactic federation."

Its energy: 10^36 watts. Its likely population: trillions.

## 2) The Question 2030B Is Afraid To Skip

Before we open the door of the galaxy, we must ask: what will we bring with us?

If we leave our planet still carrying our resentments, we will not meet galactic neighbors — we will export to them whatever makes them hate us. If we leave carrying our impatience — decisions made in milliseconds under pressure — we will ignite a galactic war over a misunderstanding that one hour of reflection would have prevented.

For this reason, Type 3 in 2030B rests on two pillars:

- **Wisdom (WDC)** — so we discern before we move.
- **Mercy (SCC)** — so we love before we interpret.

## 3) "Be Wiser" — Four Levels

*Be Wiser* divides wisdom into:

1. **Responder** — reacts to every stimulus instantly.
2. **Reflector** — holds the response for three seconds.
3. **Discerner** — sees the hidden layers before speaking.
4. **Visionary** — sees what no one has yet seen.

In our 0.7 civilization, most of us live at Level 1. We tweet before we read. We judge before we listen. We attack before we understand.

Type 3 requires that its leaders live at Level 3 at minimum. Because a single error in a galactic decision could cost twenty solar systems.

SelfAI-W (the Decision Mirror) records your decisions and shows you their consequences a month later, a year later, ten years later. When you have seen a thousand of your past decisions on a single timeline, you learn that speed of decision is not a virtue — measured patience is.

## 4) "Be Kinder" — Four Levels

*Be Kinder* divides mercy into:

1. **Empathetic** — feels the other's pain.
2. **Benefactor** — moves slightly.
3. **Servant** — makes serving others a routine.
4. **Giver** — gives when the cost is high.

In Type 3, when we meet another civilization — perhaps aquatic, perhaps plasma-based, perhaps purely digital — being empathetic will not be enough. We will need to be servants. To serve a civilization whose language we cannot read, whose music we cannot hear, whose chemistry we do not share. Yet we give them water, information, or time.

SCC, at a galactic scale, becomes the currency of cosmic diplomacy.

## 5) First Contact: 2030B's Rules for the Other Civilization

If we ever find another civilization, 2030B proposes five rules of meeting, drawn from the Covenant of Humility:

1. **We do not judge them** — because they were not created to be judged by us.
2. **We do not call them "lower" or "higher"** — they are different, neither less nor more.
3. **We begin by listening, not teaching** — because we do not know what they know.
4. **We use their language before ours** — because language is respect.
5. **We give before we ask** — because the first relationship between two civilizations is one of kindness, not of trade.

Notice anything? These are the same rules 2030B uses for your neighbor on the street. Type 3 is not a moral leap — it is a continuation of who you already are.

## 6) Wormholes and the Ethics of Passage

Type 3 supposes stable wormholes — shortcuts between stars. But every wormhole opens a door, and every door demands a passage ethic:

- Who crosses first? Not the strongest — the most trusted (TIC).
- Who guards the gate? Not the smartest — the fairest (JEC).
- Who decides when it opens and when it closes? Not the king — the Justified Commons.

In a 0.7 civilization, every wormhole becomes militarized in the first week. In a 2030B civilization, every wormhole becomes a library, or a humanitarian corridor, or a passage for refugees.

## 7) What Does a Galaxy Weigh Inside a Human Vessel?

Imagine carrying in your head the image of a hundred billion stars. Imagine knowing that there are trillions of living beings out there, some of them thinking, some of them dreaming. Do you sleep that night? Can you function in the morning?

Type 3 does not need only intelligent minds. It needs minds capable of receiving. And that — in 2030B's language — is the work of GRC. Gratitude widens the heart enough to receive an enormous gift without bursting.

Whoever has not learned to thank for an apple cannot learn to thank for a galaxy.

## 8) End of the Chapter

Type 3 is not a technical event. It is an event of conscience. If we go out into the galaxy without respecting one another on a public bus, we will not respect whoever greets us near another star.

In the next chapter: Type 4 — the Universal Civilization. We will see how *Be Fairer* and *Be Honester* — justice and honesty — converge when power becomes absolute and accountability vanishes.

May we be taught to carry the galaxy, not to be carried by it.
