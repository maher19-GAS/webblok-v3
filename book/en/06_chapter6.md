# Chapter Six — Types 5 and 6: The Multiverse and the Source Code of Reality — Read Through "Be Freer" and "Be Grateful"

> "There shall be no compulsion in religion." — Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:256
> "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." — Qur'an, Ibrahim 14:7

— — —

## 1) Type 5, Briefly

Type 5 is the civilization that crosses between multiple universes. Each universe carries different physical laws. The speed of light may not be 300,000 km per second next door. There may be no atom. Time may run backward.

Inside this civilization, you borrow solutions from advanced versions of yourself in other universes. You consult the "you" who took a different path a thousand years ago. You learn from your parallel civilization that found a cure for a cancer you have not yet found.

## 2) Type 6, Briefly

Type 6 surpasses universes themselves. It can edit cosmic laws from outside them. The constants — light speed, Planck's constant, the gravitational coupling — are no longer constants, but editable parameters.

This is a civilization that works as a "law engineer." If it wants a universe where love is stronger than gravity, it makes one. If it wants a universe without pain, it makes one. If it wants a universe with circular time, it makes one.

## 3) The Bigger Question: What Happens to the Will When Choices Are Infinite?

Here a paradox detonates that Kardashev never raised. Whoever holds unlimited choices loses the capacity to choose. Psychology calls it "choice paralysis."

In Types 5 and 6, where everything is possible, the only remaining question becomes: why do I choose this instead of that? And because the answer cannot live inside reality — every reality is available — the answer must live inside the human.

Here 2030B meets philosophy's deepest question: what remains of the human when no constraints remain upon him?

## 4) "Be Freer" — Freedom That Does Not Decay Into Absurdity

*Be Freer* divides freedom into:

1. **Bound** — wants but cannot.
2. **Resistor** — can sometimes.
3. **Reclaimer** — reclaims his decision from his addiction.
4. **Master of Self** — moves only with conscious intent.

In our 0.7 civilization, most of us live at Level 1 or 2. Addicted to a phone, to caffeine, to a like on social media, to a neighbor's glance, to salaries we do not love.

In Types 5 and 6, external freedom is unlimited — yet internal freedom becomes the only variable. The Master of Self picks good among infinite choices. The one who is not master is tested by every desire, and dies internally before his body dies.

This is the secret of "no compulsion in religion." Faith not chosen freely is worth nothing. And the additional books *Digital Liberation* and *Political Liberation* (Be Freer Books 4–5) apply this today: whoever has not freed himself from an app on his phone will not free himself in a parallel universe.

## 5) "Be Grateful" — Gratitude as an Anchor in a Sea of Possibilities

*Be Grateful* divides gratitude into:

1. **Noticer** — sees the blessing.
2. **Acknowledger** — attributes it to its source.
3. **Expresser** — says it out loud.
4. **User** — uses the blessing properly toward its purpose.

In the multiverse, gratitude becomes the only means by which the heart does not explode from too many choices. "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you" — and the verse is not about money increasing, but about capacity increasing. The grateful heart widens. The ungrateful heart narrows even when it owns everything.

In a multiverse civilization, gratitude is the difference between "a god" and "a devil." Whoever holds power and gives thanks becomes like the prophets. Whoever holds power and is ungrateful becomes the figure cosmic stories warn about.

## 6) A Short Metaphysical Story

Imagine a Type 6 human named Ibrahim. He wakes up in the morning. Three different universes stand before him; he can choose to live in any of them today.

In the first universe, his family is wealthy. He does not work. He consumes.
In the second, he is a head of state. Everyone obeys him.
In the third, he is a servant in a small restaurant, feeding the poor.

Which universe does he choose?

In a 0.7 civilization, most of us would choose the second or the first. In a mature 2030B civilization, Ibrahim chooses the third. Not because he is foolish, but because he read *Be Grateful* to its end, and learned that whoever serves earns God's face. And whoever owns may lose it.

This is the difference. And it is the difference that determines who is permitted to cross over into Type 7.

## 7) Six Before Seven: Why the Order Matters

Type 6 gives you the right to edit the laws. Type 7 — as we will see — gives you the right to rewrite them from scratch. That is a vast leap in danger. For this reason, 2030B proposes that no one crosses to Type 7 except whoever completed Type 6 with steady honor.

The honor of Type 6: you do not use the law-edit to wrong a parallel version of another human. You do not create a universe where a copy of you enslaves the others. You do not exploit the possibility of plurality to erase the face of oneness inside you.

Many imaginary civilizations in American, Chinese, and Russian literature reach Type 6 and explode within it. Not because Type 6 is dangerous in itself — but because its residents never trained on FLC and GRC starting from Type 1.

## 8) End of the Chapter

Types 5 and 6 are an exam of the will and the heart. Are you the master of yourself when no one imposes anything on you? Are you grateful when everything is possible?

In the next chapter: Type 7 — the editors of reality. We will see why 2030B considers it not the top of the ladder — but a test of the top. Then we will open an eighth door, written in no physics book — yet closer to us than all that came before.

May we be taught to choose the good when evils are open before us.
