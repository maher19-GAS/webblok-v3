# Chapter Five — Type 4: The Universal Civilization — Read Through "Be Fairer" and "Be Honester"

> "Be just; that is nearer to righteousness." — Qur'an, Al-Ma'idah 5:8
> "And be with those who are true." — Qur'an, At-Tawbah 9:119

— — —

## 1) Type 4, Briefly

Type 4 is the civilization that harvests the energy of an entire universe — hundreds of billions of galaxies. It commands dark energy. It prevents the heat death of the cosmos. It opens intergalactic wormholes. It reignites dying stars. It may possess time travel. It may seed life across galaxies with engineered comets — directed panspermia.

It is, practically, an engineer of the universe. And its civilizations are no longer limited by how, but by intention.

## 2) The Moral Question That Explodes Here

If a human — or a group of humans — holds the power of an entire universe, one question becomes more important than all that came before: who holds them accountable?

In Type 1, the law holds you accountable. In Type 2, the planet does. In Type 3, your galactic neighbors do. In Type 4, no one. Because everything is you.

For this reason, Type 4 is the peak of power's danger. The only solution, in 2030B's eyes, is to build the civilization's self-immunity against corruption before it arrives. An immunity made of two pillars:

- **Justice (JEC)** — your scales are honest with you when no one is watching.
- **Truth (TIC)** — you tell yourself the truth when no one hears you but God.

## 3) "Be Fairer" — Four Levels

*Be Fairer* divides justice into:

1. **Partial** — just sometimes.
2. **Relative** — just with those close.
3. **Trusted** — just with the stranger too.
4. **Upright** — just with one's adversary before one's friend.

In our 0.7 civilization, most of us are just with our friends. The hardest JEC test is when you are the judge, and your opponent is the one who wronged you first. A universal civilization cannot survive if two classes are born inside it: oppressors and the oppressed.

For this reason, *The Book of Major Causes* (Be Fairer — Book 4) applies the JEC scale to global cases: Palestine, Ukraine, Sudan, Yemen, the Rohingya, the Uyghurs, Congo, Kashmir. Because whoever was not just on Earth will not be just in a universe.

## 4) "Be Honester" — The Honesty Case at Universal Scale

*Be Honester* — together with its two additional books on Institutional Honesty and the Testimony of Truth — builds an explicit contract:

- No deceptive advertising.
- No reputation laundering.
- No falsified data.
- No expert hiding a flaw inside his institution.
- No witness silent before the camera when their testimony is costly.

In Type 4, where a single engineer's word can cause the birth or death of a star, honesty is not a virtue — it is infrastructure. A lie in Type 1 costs a job. A lie in Type 4 costs a galaxy.

## 5) Justice With Time: When Do We Change the Past?

Type 4 supposes we may hold time travel. This opens a question 2030B considers the most dangerous in the history of civilization: when do we change the past? Do we have the right to edit it?

The JEC scale says: no. The past is not yours to edit. And even if you could, editing another human's past is a theft of their dignity (the Full Dignity Principle of the Covenant of Humility).

For this reason, a mature Type 4 civilization travels into the past only to observe, never to intervene. And this — if true — explains why no galactic civilization has visited us yet. Perhaps they are here, watching, refusing to intervene. Not out of indifference. Out of respect.

## 6) A Transparent Story: The Institution That Testified Against Itself

The year is 2034. A large company inside the early 2030B economy discovers that its product is causing chronic anxiety in five percent of its users. Management has two choices:

- Hide the data (the 0.7 way).
- Publish it instantly on the open ledger, and compensate every affected user (the 2030B way).

Management chooses the second. The company's market value drops 30% in a day. But its collective TIC rises, and new customers flock to it. Within a year, its value is twice what it had been before.

This is not idealized virtue. It is a new economy that makes honesty profitable. And that — nothing more — is what the 2030B covenant wants: systems that reward truth instead of rewarding posture.

## 7) The Universe as a Trust

In Type 4, we become "stewards" of the universe in the full sense. "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority." A steward holds a trust he does not own, deputizing for the One who truly owns it.

Let me record for you the golden 2030B rule at this level: no one owns the universe. Everyone is a deputy for God. Only by this does Type 4 survive its own danger.

## 8) End of the Chapter

Type 4 is an exam: can you be just when no one but God holds you accountable? Can you be truthful when lying is profitable and no one will catch you?

Whoever passed the exam of the hungry beggar on the corner will pass the exam of the universe. Whoever failed the first — do not open the universe to him. He will burn it.

In the next chapter: Types 5 and 6 — the Multiverse, and the Source Code of Reality. We will see how *Be Freer* and *Be Grateful* — freedom and gratitude — meet when all choices are open, and yet the human still chooses the good.

May we be taught to be just with the universe before we are given the universe.
